## Decisions And Scope

- Keep exact objective, gradient, SPD and transactional commit checks. A sketch proposes steps only.
- Distinguish Hessian and inverse-Hessian spectra explicitly at the trust-region boundary.
- Keep stochastic model quality separate from deterministic implementation correctness; a 10% sample difference is not a certificate.
- Positive full-parameter ridge, frozen scales/center, shared M0 penalties in M1. No division by parameter count.
- M1 contains M0. Additional translation-invariant local quadratics share one representation for statistics, operators and package output.
- OHO cross-term polarity follows the frozen geometric `a0` in the specification; salt-environment polarity is not part of this M1 candidate.
- Preserve energy allocation, reverse images, center balancing and internal mass-COM conventions.
- A0/A1 compare at fixed initial tau; B/C retain staged barriers and final strict gates.
- Already inspected holdout data are development data. Missing independent data or CUDA validation must fail closed for acceptance.
- All branches share a cumulative budget; no budget reset on a branch change or restart.
- No commits, pushes, new material trajectories or reference acceptance claims in this implementation turn.
