``heat_current_rpmd_ja.out``
============================

Produced when ``rpmd_ja on`` is enabled, and appended after the run. Columns are time in ps, the three centroid-current components, the three correction components, and the three total JA-current components. Current values use eV Angstrom per natural time unit. Each segment records backend, model fingerprint, reference temperature, cell volume, sample interval, and frame count.
