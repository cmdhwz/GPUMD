# Independent quantum and RPMD validation

Current source is NOT RUN after the user's static-only direction. `spectrum.solve` whitens the fixed-A mass metric and uses the existing padded HO Weyl construction and independent periodic Fourier DVR. Its primary 2x2 blocks are direct physical J1/J3, not a reallocation of finite commutator error. It separately saves the finite-matrix i[H,PE]/hbar curve and mu2, and full, interior and thermal-weighted discrepancies. For affine r and constant G, the infinite-space Weyl symbol of the instantaneous current is exactly {PE,H}; higher odd Moyal terms vanish. The hbar² Moyal source belongs to the generator of J3. Finite-matrix discrepancy is representation/truncation error, not a physical J1 correction. No spectral convergence or quantum acceptance is claimed.

`run_controls.py` defaults to the frozen cases' full time grid, basis sequence and HMC settings; `--quick` is an explicitly labelled smoke profile. HMC dt and leapfrog count are distinct from real-time dt. For each origin one trajectory carries J1,J3 and both fixed-reference corrections for each available well. It saves raw arrays `(groups,origins,times,channels)`, labels, timestamps and source/config contract, and uncentered ordered group cross correlations. Fixed-A unequal-endpoint H/D variants share reference masses and endpoint masses; the historical D case remains separate. Groups start at alternating stable minima where two exist; sampling/mixing is not established by this setup.

`analyze.py <run-directory> <case-variant> --quantum <label> --output <new-directory>` verifies contracts, dimensions, labels and raw-flow reconstruction. It outputs physical 11/13/31/33 and cc/cDelta/Deltac/DeltaDelta blocks, finite-window integrals, a forward mu2 diagnostic, and a paired whole-group bootstrap of RMS(mean candidate curve)-RMS(mean centroid curve) with fixed configurable seed and count. This descriptive interval does not certify independent origins or convergence. Ranking is UNKNOWN until basis, box, bead count, timestep, sampling and mixing gates pass.

The optional `--u9-config tests/u9_oho/general_u9_example.json` adds a general nine-coefficient case without replacing the six frozen cases. Fields are c[5], K, lambda[3], reference_masses {ML,m,MR}, isotope_masses[3], d0, beta, hbar, units and source. The collinear fixed-A embedding is derived from reference masses; isotope masses change its metric. A single well has a single reference. This finite-model input is distinct from native diagnostic configuration and supplies no bulk Cartesian operator.
